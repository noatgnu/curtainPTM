About FDR Curves

In normal mode, significance uses the p-value and fold change cutoffs and a pasted curve is only drawn. In curve mode, points on or above the curve are significant and volcano groups are labelled above or below the curve.

Note: A curve replaces the standard cutoff lines on the volcano plot.
